## Description (4,000 characters at most)

```text
MonÉlu shows how the deputies of the French Assemblée nationale vote, scrutin by scrutin, in plain French.
An independent service, not affiliated with the Assemblée nationale.
The app is in French.

YOUR DEPUTY
• Find your deputy from your address or postal code, and follow them from the home screen.
• Each week, the home screen shows the Assemblée's main votes and how the deputies you follow voted.
• A deputy's profile lists their votes, their presence at scrutins and the votes where they broke with their group.
• Compare the votes of two deputies.

EXPLORE
• Every public scrutin since 1 July 2025, with the official result, the hemicycle and each group's position.
• The bills voted on and their progress, the week's agenda, themes, parliamentary groups and departments.

THE QUIZ
• Which deputy votes like you?
• Answer for, against or abstain on real scrutins, then see which deputies voted the way you did.

ASK
• Ask a question about votes and deputies: the answer cites its sources.
• Paste a claim about a deputy's vote: it is checked against the official scrutins and gets a verdict (true, false, misleading or unverifiable), with the scrutins it cites.
• These answers are AI-assisted and labelled as such: the official scrutin is the reference.

THE DATA
• Official open data of the Assemblée nationale, reused under the Licence Ouverte 2.0.
• Updated every working day.
• Every scrutin links to its official source.
• Plain-language summaries are written by AI from the official text, and labelled as such.

YOUR PRIVACY
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• The deputies and themes you follow stay on your iPhone.
```
